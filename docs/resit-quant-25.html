How does victimisation relate to trust in the police?

The European Social Survey dataset for Bulgaria was downloaded to answer this assignment question.

In this document, you will find outputs from a series of steps constituting a simple statistical analysis workflow to address the set assignment question on the Bulgarian sample.

The outputs are not annotated, but they should be very familiar to you from the workshops. Please engage with the module worksheets to work your way through the interpretation of the findings.

Remember: the Edit Data menu is where you can find the variable names; a variable search function and links to the original survey questionnaires and codebooks are available at https://soc2069-quant-25.chrismoreh.com/Data/
